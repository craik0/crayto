EARL BY EARL SWEATSHIRT [2010]

1.0 this is for archival purposes only, if there are any problems please reach out to me on X and I will resolve them

EARL is the debut mixtape by Earl Sweatshirt, released in 2010 when he was just 16 years old. The project introduced the world to Earl's dark, introspective lyricism and complex wordplay that would define his career. Raw, unfiltered, and visceral, EARL captures a teenage prodigy grappling with depression, violence, and nihilism over lo-fi production. This archive preserves the mixtape in its best available quality.
