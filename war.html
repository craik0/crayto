WAR BY YE [2022-2024] 🕊️

this is for archival purposes only, if there are any problems please reach out to me on X and I will reslove them

WAR is an unreleased Ye album developed alongside producer James Blake. Though it never officially dropped, tracks have slowly leaked and premiered between 2022–2024. While there are thousands of unreleased projects in Ye’s catalog, WAR stands out — it captures an artist publicly processing grief, spiraling mental health, and reckoning with his place in his career. I went ahead and complied the tracks that are in a good state. Please enjoy and feel free to tell me if I missed any good tracks.
