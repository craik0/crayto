WAR BY YE [2022-2024] 🕊️

1.0 this is for archival purposes only, if there are any problems please reach out to me on X and I will reslove them

WAR is an unreleased album by Ye, developed alongside producer James Blake and his mentor No I.D. Though it never officially released, tracks slowly leaked and premiered between 2022–2024. While there are thousands of unreleased projects across Ye’s catalog, WAR stands out — capturing an artist publicly processing grief, spiraling mental health, and reckoning with his career. Some even consider it Ye’s final true “album.” I’ve compiled the tracks currently in the best state; if higher-quality versions or additional leaks surface, I’ll update this page accordingly. Thank you — and enjoy.
